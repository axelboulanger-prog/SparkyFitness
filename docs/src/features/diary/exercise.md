# Exercise Tracking

SparkyFitness allows you to easily track your exercise routines, including details like duration, intensity, sets, reps, weight, and calories burned. You can also import and sync your exercise routines from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health using the [Mobile App](/mobile-app/mobile-app).

For high-intensity conditioning, interval timers, and CrossFit-style WOD scoring, check out the dedicated [Interval, Circuit & WOD Workouts](/features/exercises/interval-wod-workouts) guide.
